Customer stories

Proven results across Nigerian supply chains.

The businesses we move, in their own words. Different sectors, same outcome: cargo cleared and moving.

Energy & Solar

Solar hubs and battery packs, cleared for off-grid rollout.

Jaza Energy builds solar-powered hubs that rent portable battery packs to households living beyond the grid. Every hub begins life as an import.

Solar equipment and lithium battery packs arrive as regulated cargo, so classification and documentation have to be right long before the vessel berths. Royal Purple Express handles the clearance and the inland leg out to the hub locations, so installation teams receive complete consignments rather than part-deliveries they cannot commission.
Oil & Gas

Offshore project equipment, alongside before the vessel sails.

Hydrodive runs diving, ROV and subsea construction services across Nigerian and West African waters, where one late component can hold an entire vessel.

Subsea spares, diving systems and project equipment move on schedules set by vessel windows, not by paperwork. Royal Purple Express prepares the customs documentation ahead of arrival and coordinates movement between Lagos, Onne and Port Harcourt so equipment reaches the quay in time for mobilisation.
Technology & Procurement

High-value IT and networking equipment, handled end to end.

Cinqtech supplies IT, networking and engineering procurement to clients across oil and gas, banking and construction.

Servers, networking hardware and engineering components are high in value, easily damaged and usually tied to a project deadline. Royal Purple Express clears and delivers them under secure handling, with documentation prepared in advance so equipment is not left sitting at the port collecting charges.
Manufacturing

Raw material into Kano, without stopping the extrusion lines.

Fas Agro manufactures woven polypropylene sacks and packaging from its plant in Kano, supplying Nigeria's agricultural and commercial sectors.

Polymer raw material lands at the Lagos ports and then has to travel the length of the country before it reaches the factory floor. Royal Purple Express handles clearance at the port and the inland haulage north, scheduling deliveries around the production plan rather than the port's convenience.
Marine & Dredging

Dredging and marine equipment delivered into the Niger Delta.

Lead Ultimate Resources provides marine, dredging and engineering services to the oil and gas industry across the Niger Delta.

Dredging plant, marine spares and engineering consignments are heavy, oversized and frequently urgent. Royal Purple Express plans the routing and permits for the low-bed movements and runs the clearance alongside it, so equipment arrives at the project site ready to work.
Electric Mobility

Electric motorcycles and batteries, rolled out state by state.

Spiro operates one of Africa's largest electric motorcycle and battery-swapping networks, and entered Nigeria through Ogun State.

Electric motorcycles arrive alongside lithium batteries and swap-station equipment, a mix of general and dangerous goods cargo that has to clear together to be of any use. Royal Purple Express manages the classification, clearance and onward distribution to each new hub as the network expands.
Gas & Transport

CNG conversion kits and cylinders, delivered to the centres that fit them.

Nigerian Gas & Transportation Solutions converts commercial vehicles to run on compressed natural gas, supporting Nigeria's shift to cheaper fuel.

Conversion kits and CNG cylinders are pressure equipment, which means regulated documentation and careful handling from port to workshop. Royal Purple Express clears the consignments and moves them out to the conversion centres, so fitting capacity is never left waiting on stock.
