Built to make Nigerian trade move.

Royal Purple Express International Limited exists for one reason: to take the friction out of importing, exporting and clearing goods, so businesses can grow without fighting the ports.

Our story

A logistics company built on solutions.

Royal Purple Express International Limited is a full-service logistics and freight forwarding company based in Lagos, Nigeria. We specialise in Clearing & Forwarding, Export, Import, and Haulage & Distribution across all major Nigerian ports and inland destinations.

With our wealth of experience and versatile knowledge of customs regulations, we manage your cargo completely from origin to destination: booking with shipping lines and airlines, documentation, customs clearance and final delivery. We handle sea and air shipments at Onne, Apapa and Tin Can ports and MMIA Lagos, with NCS documentation, duty payments and regulatory inspections by SON, NAFDAC and NDLEA for fast release.

With deep expertise in NCS, SON, NAFDAC, NEPC and CBN regulations, we help businesses move cargo faster, cheaper, and with zero compliance headaches, managing the entire supply chain for both sea freight and airfreight clearance.

In simple terms: we handle all the logistics so you don’t have to.

Where we are going

Our mission and vision.

Our Vision

To be recognized as Nigeria’s leading freight forwarding and customs brokerage service provider, with unparalleled customer service.

We picture a country where importers and exporters move goods with the same confidence they run the rest of their business: clearance that is quick, costs that are clear, and cargo that arrives when it should.

Our Mission

To provide quality service delivery at cost-effective prices, with a high level of integrity, to our customers irrespective of size.

We clear, move and deliver our clients' cargo with speed, transparency and genuine care, pairing licensed expertise at the ports with our own fleet and warehousing, so businesses can focus on growth instead of chasing containers.
