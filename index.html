Who we are

An integrated supply chain partner, built for Nigerian trade.

Royal Purple Express International Limited is a full-service logistics and freight forwarding company based in Lagos. We manage your cargo from origin to destination, booking, documentation, clearance and delivery. In simple terms: we handle all the logistics so you don’t have to.

  • Licensed clearing & forwarding agents at all major Nigerian ports
  • One point of contact from port arrival to final delivery
  • Owned haulage fleet and bonded warehousing capacity

What we do

Every link in your supply chain, under one roof.

Pick a single service or hand us the whole journey. Either way, the cargo moves and you stay informed.

01

Clearing & ForwardingCustoms cleared, fast

Licensed agents at Apapa, Tin Can, Onne and the airports. Pre-classification and accurate documentation secure release before demurrage eats your margin, with import and export programmes handled end to end.

 02

Haulage & DistributionPort to doorstep, own fleet

A modern fleet of flatbeds, lowbeds and curtain-side trucks carries your cargo from the port gate to your doorstep, Port Harcourt to Lagos to Abuja and beyond. Insured, escorted where needed, one point of contact throughout.

 03

WarehousingBonded space, accurate stock

Bonded storage, inventory management and value-added services across 19 distribution centres, so your stock lands, sits safely, and ships when you need it.

Also: sea & air freight, project cargo and oversized moves, handled by a dedicated team.
