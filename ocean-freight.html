Overview

Volume, moved economically.

When cost matters more than speed, the sea is your lane. From a single full container to consolidated cargo sharing space, ocean freight carries the heavy volume that keeps businesses supplied.

We book the right service for your cargo, then handle the part most forwarders leave to you: clearing it at the Nigerian port and trucking it inland to your door on our own fleet.

How we help

Sea freight, end to end.

Full container (FCL)

A container dedicated to your cargo, booked on the right lane and timed around your supply schedule.

Shared container (LCL)

Smaller shipments consolidated with others so you only pay for the space you use, without waiting for a full load.

Port handling & clearance

Customs release at the terminal and onward haulage, so your container does not sit racking up demurrage.

Why Royal Purple Express

Built to deliver for Ocean Freight.

The ocean leg is only half the job. The value is in what happens when the container lands. Here is how we cover it.

Licensed at the ports

One coordinator, port to door

A single point of contact owns the booking, the clearance and the delivery, so nothing falls between hand-offs.

Owned fleet and bonded space

We truck cargo inland on our own vehicles and hold it in our own bonded and general warehousing.
